# Designing the Verse Card for Real Screens

The Verse Card was redesigned as a responsive composition rather than a fixed desktop picture. The quotation adapts to the available width, long Scripture passages receive smaller typography, and the header and footer resize with the card.

The Local and Jerusalem date-time block is centered. Branding remains on the left, window controls remain on the right, and the footer controls stay anchored at the bottom. Dedicated Verse Card A− and A+ controls let a reader make a final adjustment without leaving the card.

The PC DPI scale, PC window resolution, Verse Card text size, and devotional text size are independent settings. This keeps a change made for a monitor from unexpectedly changing the reading typography or the other application surface.
