# Windows 1.4.4: Display Scaling and the Verse Card

Work Day with God Windows 1.4.4 adds PC display scaling from 65% through 150%, independent PC window resolution choices for 720p, 1080p, 1440p, and 4K UHD, and responsive sizing across the Verse Card and devotional reader.

The Verse Card has its own text-size setting and direct A−/A+ controls. Its quotation, header, clock, greeting, buttons, footer, and status text adapt to the available card width. DPI and resolution remain separate: choosing 1920 × 1080 keeps the actual window at 1920 × 1080 rather than multiplying that resolution by the DPI preference.

Windows 1.4.4 is available from the [official GitHub release](https://github.com/mcographics/WorkDaywithGod/releases/tag/v1.4.4). The installer is unsigned; verify its published checksum before installation.
